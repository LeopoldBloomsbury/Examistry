Free CPA Starter Guide

A concrete first-two-weeks plan for candidates who are tired of opening more CPA tabs and still not knowing what to do tomorrow.

The before/after

Before: scattered resources, vague section anxiety, and a calendar filled with passive review. After: one primary stack, a 14-day startup sequence, and a clear decision rule for when to move into AUD Quickstart.

Start with direction

  • Pick one primary resource stack.
  • Choose your section based on momentum and constraints.
  • Schedule your first recall block before you schedule more volume.

Your first 14 days

  • Day 1: section decision and calendar reset.
  • Days 2-5: concept review plus same-day recall.
  • Days 6-10: one warm-up drill block and one review block.
  • Days 11-14: refine weak points instead of adding more sources.

Recommended next step

If audit flow, reports, and evidence questions still feel shapeless after setup, make AUD Quickstart your first paid step. Use the 30-Day Rescue Pack only when the exam date is already tight.
