Free CPA Starter Guide

What to study first, what to ignore, and how to structure your first 14 days.

Start with direction

  • Pick one primary resource stack.
  • Choose your section based on momentum and constraints.
  • Schedule your first recall block before you schedule more volume.

Your first 14 days

  • Day 1: section decision and calendar reset.
  • Days 2-5: concept review plus same-day recall.
  • Days 6-10: one warm-up drill block and one review block.
  • Days 11-14: refine weak points instead of adding more sources.

Recommended next step

Move into a focused section pack or the 30-Day Rescue Pack depending on how close your exam date is.
